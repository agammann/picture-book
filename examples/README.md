# Picture Book examples

These two short adaptations demonstrate the full read, edit, illustrate, and export workflow. Both preserve the beginning, central journey, and ending of their source stories.

| Book | Original author | Format | Reading experience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Alice's Adventures in Wonderland](alice-in-wonderland/book.pdf) | Lewis Carroll | 24 pages, 12 illustrations | A playful journey through Wonderland, ending with Alice waking beside her sister. |
| [The Time Machine](the-time-machine/book.pdf) | H. G. Wells | 24 pages, 12 illustrations | A journey into the future, including loss, return, a final disappearance, and the two flowers left behind. Contains non-graphic danger and loss. |

## Open an editable copy

Download `book.picturebook.json` from either folder. In Picture Book, choose **New book → Choose a file** and select it. The story, characters, scene directions, and artwork are editable. No generation account is needed to read, edit, or export an existing example.

## How these were prepared

The original works were first published in 1865 and 1895. The source PDFs were imported locally. The generated drafts were manually revised for simpler language, chronology, continuity, and the actual ending. Illustrations were reviewed alongside the text, and scenes with misplaced characters were revised. These are reviewed examples, not a guarantee that every automatic draft is ready to publish.

The original uploaded PDFs and their extracted full text are not distributed. The example projects contain newly written adaptation prose and illustrations. `source.text` is empty. Short source notes identify narrative episodes without claiming page numbers in the uploaded editions.
