# Picture Book design

Reference: `work/art/picture-book-editor-concept.png` in the development workspace.

The working surface is an editorial book studio. Warm ivory (#f7f5ef), dark ink (#242b32), cobalt (#2c50df), thin gray rules. Georgia/serif controls and book text follow the concept. Accessible sans-serif is reserved for small utility status text. Header height 66px, left physical page rail 116px, inspector 340px, flexible center canvas. The book is the visual focus: full-bleed art on the left, spacious story page on the right. Controls are native HTML with Lucide outline icons. Navigation, import, library, settings, reading, and exports share the same typography and square-to-soft 4px corners.

First-screen copy: Picture Book; My library; New book; Settings; Read; Export; Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland; 24 pages; Story; Art; Page title; Story; Illustration; Create illustration. Functional additions permitted where required: Characters, saved status, and accessible labels. On narrow screens the rail becomes a horizontal strip and the inspector follows the book. No separate marketing page.

Required states: showcase editor; source import and adaptation options; local library; connection settings; art/character inspector; generation progress and cancel; reading mode; export. All mutation controls must work and all generation failures must preserve user work.
